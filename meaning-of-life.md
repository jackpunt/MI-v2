
# The Meaning of Life, how life gets meaning.

In the laws of physics there is no equation, formula or symbol that is associated with "meaning" (nor "life").

"Meaning" is a thing that arises from our brain, it is not intrinsic to the Universe.

Likewise, "Life" is a construct created by the brain, a useful category of things that eat, move, procreate, die.
Which defines the category of what we call "living things" (until they die).

The "life" category was easier and more clear a thousand years ago, before we delved into bio-chemistry.
And it's "bio" because "biology" is the science word associated with "living things".
If the brains over the ages had not created and identifcation or concept of "life", maybe it would all just be "chemistry".
It gets tricky when it gets down to the small things (virus) or large/composite things (ecosystems).
This paragraph just to emphasize that "life" is a man-made concept, synthesized by our learning/categorizing brain.

So the short answer for "what is the meaning of life" is: whatever your brain/mind decides it to be.
Which is influenced by what you see, hear, read. 
There are whole realms of institutions created to create and promulgate that influence.
We call them variously: religion, science, politics, philosophy, etc.

What someone attibutes to "the meaning of life" is mostly a reflection of how their brain/mind has been influenced by those institutions. None of them are particularly "right"; they mostly provide made-up answers to made-up questions.
Of the batch mentioned, philosophy and it's sub-domain science give somewhat more useful or durable answers.
Which tend to be in agreement with the central-core of durable religions, and make a reasonable foundation for government.
Although politics tends to filter for more immediate personal benefits to the polititian.
