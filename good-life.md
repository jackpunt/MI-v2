# Good Life

A good life, personal philosophy, success.

Define "Success" (in life) as Happy && Productive.

The meaning of Happy is the usual and obvious; each person knows when they are happy.
A direct connection to endorphin release in the brain.
But striving for only happy is a worthless life, leads to addiction, self-absorbtion, lacking benefit to society.
Needs to be balanced with 'productive', contributing to the larger picture; family, clan, culture, society, perhaps even so term of history.

Productive 
Return some value, reduce entropy, leave things a bit better than you found them.
