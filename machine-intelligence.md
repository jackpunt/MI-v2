# Machine Intelligence

## Definitions

The root of intelligence; what it is, why it is:
To enhance survival, of the specimen and species/genes.

There are several adapations for survival evolved in nature,
but brains and intelligence evolved primarily to predict the future.
A necessary prerequiste is to remember the past,
and to recognize when and to what extent the present matches the past.

That is our basis for defining intelligence.

Note in particular, that intelligence is not specifically evolved to do math, play games, solve puzzles, or generate language. 
Those are derived *after* intelligence.
