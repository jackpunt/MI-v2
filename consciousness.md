# Consciousness

Attributes:
* decisions
* awareness
* memory: logging & recall

The brain is constantly predicting the future and deciding on actions to bring the future state to a desired goal state.

Brain has many sub-nets working in parallel monitoring sensory/enteroceptive state and taking corrective action.
Many and perhaps most of that can be done without conflict between the independent subsystems.
At some point, develop a uber-control mode (feed, fight, flee, freeze, mate, etc) to resolve.
With more development, there will be more choices, and decisions will require more subtle differentiated choices.

## origination 1:

- the whole story of Input-Process-Output as state-machine. (for any given input/stimulus state there is a response and a next-state)
- 'state' vector include (external) sensory and (internal) proprioceptive/enteroceptive, and (later) goals/objectives.
- 'output' side is all the glands, muscles, and (later) mental actions.
- massive matrix & table lookup is sufficient (log *everything*, every instant is a row in the state table)
- the historical table is a vast DFA, can be used to predict future states; IF one can match the current state to the previous/close instances in the table.
- vastly inefficient, there are obvious commonalities that can be identified, cross product of sub-machines.
- compress by generalizing across rows (row X is almost exactly like row Y, and the response is essentially the same)
- optimize by partitioning on sensory modality
- optimize by partitioning (modeling) objects; entities, agents (sub-machines)
- entities: a leaf or rock, physical rigidity, permanence (passive and active)
- agents: active entities, more varied behaviour. (prey & predator types; also consspecifics, mates, offspring) 
- optimize by generalization across similar agents (saving encoding space)
- now the predictive 'next-state' lookup considers the entities that are relevant/in-scope 
- eventually there will be a sub-machine the encapsulates *this* entity, that is: the **self** 
- or the whole compressed historical state-machine is the self model, and in the place where a self-agent sub-machine would exist there is a pointer/reference to the larger table. the dreaded recursion! but that is ok, it doesn't have to be an infinite loop.

"Consciousness" as the result of the logging (later with narrative), awareness (recognizing that self reference) and decision (use the self-awareness and self-goals to evaluate/arbitrate decision on plan of action)

## Origination 2:

Define the term "consciousness": the story we tell ourselves, the continuing narrative of what is happening, what we are "thinking", how we plan, choose, decide.

### layers:
biochemical layer: neurotransmitters, proton pumps, activation potential... 

neural/cellular layer: dentrites, axons, glials, etc. signaling and pathways (billions of cells, 100s B connections)

neural networks: the mesh of connected cells; compute, learn; input->output (but 'multi')

clusters: portions (fuzzy) of the network that monitor some input, predict, assert some output.  clusters may monitor similar inputs and compete to drive the effective output.

clusters can from heirarchys and loops. feedback, self-sustaining/self-regulating.

### the story:
simple organisms can sense and react; self-regulate, find food, shelter, mates, etc.
(no need for organization, language, consciousness)

collective organisms communicate (eg: chemiclly) to maintain/regulate the hoard.
(biofilms, etc) evolve into multi-organ entities?

...  animals as we know them ...

individuals still most sensing & deciding on their own

herd/flock: (saftey in numbers) sensing the flow of the school/flock, staying with the crowd. signaling is implicit.

leadership emerges: sensing focuses on one or a few "alpha" deciders, follow-the-leader. Note that signalling (of direction/intent) is sufficient; negotiation/explanation/persuasion is not required, classic autocracy: the leader decides and the herd follows as directed. 

pluralism: ?  multiple potential leaders, the need to choose, negotiate, rationalize.  That is: the need for a language of communication. signals that supply more that mere direction.

language begins the production of history and logic, to remember and explain.

the brains co-evolve to process language, representing others' state (constraints, goals, intentions, reasons, threats, promises, etc)
to survive in a social/language system, individuals learn to communicate beyond signalling actions, and express their state, and understand the state of others.
Another layer of clusters to monitor their own state and represent the state of others.

To produce language and express their own state, that brain needs to recognize its internal state and serialize that into output behaviours that express that state.
That facility (eventually) operates autonommously/automatically, a mostly continuous generation of a story explaining the internal state, the inputs attended to, the choices available, the decisions made. This is/becomes "consciousness".

A key feature of this: consciousness is not the internal state, it is not the decider of choices and actions, it is the transscipt recording the states, choices and decisions of (the lower layer of) clusters which continue to operate autonomously.

### similar motivation internally and socially:
Seems like 'magic' that social organization and language would be the driver for such a thing? 
Assert that the same forces would drive internal development (sans language?) of the same mechanisms.
That is: when there are singular clusters that drive behavior, something must choose which mode wins, the brain will need a decider cluster for that competition. 
It's possible (although maybe unnecessary, and maybe unlikely) that the internal systems create a signalling/language and means of representing the alternative choices which can be analyzed and evaluated by a decider. the same forces that drive the evolution of language and leadership in a society of individuals could drive the evolution of internal systems for similar reasons.

### language & consciousness
Sure, there can be awareness and decision making without language.
(although per above, there may be internal signaling/representation/proto-language)
Asserting here (repeating form defition above) that the phenomenon of consciousness is not only detected and expressed by language, but is created as a side-effect of solving social communication and community decision making. (still considering that external/group society and internal society-of-mind have some similar constraints; with a chicken/egg and co-evolution possibilities)

the basic mechanism:

whenever there are mulitple proposers or proposals, there must be a decision.
a simple solution is "might makes right": the proposal with the strongest signal, presumably representing the strongest evidence or best historical outcomes or whatever metric is producing/evaluating the signal strength, is chosen.
(as above: this simple autocratic solution is easy and obvious; and likely brains and societies evolve to support its implementation)

when the decisions spaces get more comples, the more detailed representations of goals and constraints leads to better outcomes. those more representations co-evolve with advanced signalling and language. our thesis is that consciousness is so much the intiator of language and social communication, but rather the interalization of the language and communication necessary in 'social' organisms. noting that 'social' group size and organization can be as simple as family, clan, tribe. (later maybe we can consider the language and consciousness of hives of ants and bees...)
