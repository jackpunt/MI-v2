# Consciousness

Attributes:
* decisions
* awareness
* memory: logging & recall

The brain is constantly predicting the future and deciding on actions to bring the future state to a desired goal state.

Brain has many sub-nets working in parallel monitoring sensory/enteroceptive state and taking corrective action.
Many and perhaps most of that can be done without conflict between the independent subsystems.
At some point, develop a uber-control mode (feed, fight, flee, freeze, mate, etc) to resolve.
With more development, there will be more choices, and decisions will require more subtle differentiated choices.

origination:

- the whole story of Input-Process-Output as state-machine. (for any given input/stimulus state there is a response and a next-state)
- 'state' vector include (external) sensory and (internal) proprioceptive/enteroceptive, and (later) goals/objectives.
- 'output' side is all the glands, muscles, and (later) mental actions.
- massive matrix & table lookup is sufficient (log *everything*, every instant is a row in the state table)
- the historical table is a vast DFA, can be used to predict future states; IF one can match the current state to the previous/close instances in the table.
- vastly inefficient, there are obvious commonalities that can be identified, cross product of sub-machines.
- compress by generalizing across rows (row X is almost exactly like row Y, and the response is essentially the same)
- optimize by partitioning on sensory modality
- optimize by partitioning (modeling) objects; entities, agents (sub-machines)
- entities: a leaf or rock, physical rigidity, permanence (passive and active)
- agents: active entities, more varied behaviour. (prey & predator types; also consspecifics, mates, offspring) 
- optimize by generalization across similar agents (saving encoding space)
- now the predictive 'next-state' lookup considers the entities that are relevant/in-scope 
- eventually there will be a sub-machine the encapsulates *this* entity, that is: the **self** 
- or the whole compressed historical state-machine is the self model, and in the place where a self-agent sub-machine would exist there is a pointer/reference to the larger table. the dreaded recursion! but that is ok, it doesn't have to be an infinite loop.

"Consciousness" as the result of the logging (later with narrative), awareness (recognizing that self reference) and decision (use the self-awareness and self-goals to evaluate/arbitrate decision on plan of action)
