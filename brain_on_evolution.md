# Brain accelerates evolution

Evolutionary progress, discontinuities
- brain: learn & teach
- language
- writing

Once brain is sufficiently advanced, selection/fitness is less determined by genetic variation (although that continues) than be informational factors. 

Brains can learn, conspecifics can teach, teaching is enhanced by symbols/language.
The unit of development moves from the individual to the clan/tribe.
The spread/transmission of informational traits (knowledge or 'meme') is independent of genetic mutations and hybrid reproduction.
'Memes' are maintained in the tribe, and transmit when tribes intersect.
Much faster, more reliable than genetic/hybridization (where ramdom/statistical crossovers can hit or miss, need critical mass to succeed)
A tribe with a meme can transmit to many other tribes, a tribe can gain memes from many other tribes.

Language:
demonstration/observation, miming, sign language, symbols and 'grammar'

Written:
Maybe starts with tools (direct objects) and art (tokens) leading to ideograms.

Each level of development drives evolution radically faster.
